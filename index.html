METAL FOIL INC.  ·  BAYTOWN, TEXAS

Precision without borders.

Established manufacturing. Present in America.

Metal Foil Inc. is the U.S.-based selling arm of Jindal Metals & Alloys Limited, a manufacturer of precision stainless steel foils and strips in India and a subsidiary of Jindal SAW Limited.

We connect North American and global buyers with technical capabilities, foil inventory, quotation support and responsive customer coordination from the energy capital of the world, Houston, TX.

MANUFACTURING

Precision processes behind every foil order.
