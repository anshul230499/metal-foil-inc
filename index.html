MANUFACTURING CAPABILITY

From cold rolling to finished slit coil.

JMA combines precision rolling and downstream finishing to support thin-gauge dimensional control, surface condition and coil configuration.

01

Precision Cold Rolling

6-Hi and 20-Hi rolling capability, including ultra-thin stainless production down to 0.03 mm.

02

Bright Annealing

Horizontal and vertical bright-annealing routes for controlled surface and metallurgical condition.

03

Degreasing & Leveling

Cleaning, tension leveling and skin-pass capability for flatness, surface and downstream handling.

04

Slitting & Winding

Precision width conversion with slit, deburred, round and mill-edge options, plus oscillating winding capability.

END-USE FOCUS

Designed into demanding systems.

Precision foil becomes application-specific once temperature, corrosion, forming, electrical function, cleanliness and repeatability are defined.

A

Automotive

Heat shields, catalytic-converter components, gaskets, bellows and thermal management.

D

Defence & Aerospace

Thin metallic insulation, shielding, high-temperature assemblies and precision components.

Medical

Precision strip and foil for surgical, diagnostic and compact metal components.
