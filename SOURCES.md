### Certification library
The site links to and previews the nine certificate/policy documents represented in the JMA certification section:
1. ISSDA Certificate
2. ISO 14001 Certificate
3. ISO 9001 Certificate
4. ISO 45001 Certificate
5. IMS Policy
6. Scope of License 15997
7. Scope of License IS 2507
8. Scope of License IS 6911
9. Quality Policy

The document previews are embedded from Google Docs Viewer using JMA's linked Cloudinary originals. The documents remain JMA documents; the website does not present them as certificates issued directly to Metal Foil Inc.

## MFI logo
The MFI logo in this package is an original site asset created for Metal Foil Inc. It uses a folded stainless-ribbon monogram with navy, brushed-steel and orange accents to communicate precision foil without reproducing Jindal's logo or wordmark.
